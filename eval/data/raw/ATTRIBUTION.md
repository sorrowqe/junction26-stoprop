# Data sources in this folder

## semeval_sample.csv
100 paragraphs sampled (seed 2026) from the training split of the PTC corpus of
**SemEval-2020 Task 11: Detection of Propaganda Techniques in News Articles**
(G. Da San Martino, A. Barrón-Cedeño, H. Wachsmuth, R. Petrov, P. Nakov, 2020).
Source: Zenodo record 3952415, https://zenodo.org/records/3952415 - licensed **CC BY 4.0**.
Changes: paragraphs extracted from articles, offsets made paragraph-relative, technique labels
mapped to the Stoprop taxonomy (see `eval/semeval_sample.py`); paragraphs containing unmapped or
merged classes were skipped. Regenerate with `python eval/semeval_sample.py`.

## synthetic_gold.csv, symmetry.csv
Generated by `eval/make_synthetic.py` with a non-Claude model (default `gpt-5.5-eu`) from seeded
specs; all people, parties and places are fictional. Each row needs human review (`ok` column).

- 2026-11-14 Reported speech needs a SPECIFIC source (validate.py only, so cached model outputs stay valid; the tagger prompt is unchanged): indirect speech counts only if the speaker is a name (capitalised mid-sentence, or 'Lira Voss'/'Voss' at sentence start), a definite singular role ('the minister', 'the association') or he/she; unnamed groups ('Supporters argued', 'senior healers say') and quantified/vague sources ('many', 'several', 'experts', 'insiders', 'they') are the author's. The author's clause after a contrast ('…, but can we trust…') is never reported. Direct quotes count unless the attribution is vague. - 2026-11-14 Swarm T: MinHash LSH (128 perms, LSH threshold 0.45) only proposes candidates; the exact Jaccard of the 5-gram shingle sets decides (>= 0.6). Exact duplicates are linked by a spanning set of pairs (no O(n^2) blow-up). URLs and @mentions are removed before comparing; hashtags are kept.
- 2026-11-14 Swarm S uses scipy.stats.poisson.sf with the batch rate = posts / max(span, 1 h) and a minimum window of 60 s (timestamps are often rounded to minutes). Cadence regularity is supporting evidence only, not the S family.

- 2026-11-14 Swarm innocent patterns: headline sharing = >= 50% of posts link the same mainstream outlet and the URL slug matches the text (>= 50% of slug words) or the posts are short (median <= 25 words); short/generic = median <= 10 words or <= 3 content words after stopwords and a small courtesy vocabulary ("thanks for sharing"); quoted statement = >= 50% of posts with quotation marks + a reporting verb; event hashtag = a hashtag in >= 50% of the cluster's posts that, within +-1 h, is used by >= 3x as many accounts as the cluster has, or by >= 3 overlapping clusters. With no S and no A -> organic_looking, otherwise one level down.

        Before drawing conclusions, consider:

          Check the claims

          Looks up to 3 factual claims in recent news: did the cited source really report it, do independent outlets report it, does anything contradict it? Takes up to 30 seconds; 10 checks per hour. Results say what was found, never “true” or “false”.

          Legend

            Colours describe how a passage is written, never whether it is true. Most text stays uncoloured. There is no single “propaganda score”.

            01The tools

            Four tools, one question each.

            Each one answers a different question about what you are reading, and shows the evidence it used.

            1. 01 Analyze How is this text trying to persuade me? Names the technique, span by span, and explains each one in plain language. ↑
            2. 02 Check a source Where does this site come from? Provenance & link-network receipts: domain age, look-alike names, archive history. →
            3. 03 Swarm mode Is this crowd moving on its own? Spots coordinated waves & traces spread, from a batch of posts and never from a single one. →
            4. 04 Clean traffic Who is really visiting my site? Server-log bots, Shodan & .htaccess rules, with real visitors and verified crawlers protected. →

            02How we keep it honest

            We label how something is said. Never who said it, and never which side they are on.

            The AI proposes. Plain code verifies every quote against your text, character for character.

            Words quoted from a named source are not held against the author.

            A crowd is called coordinated only when two independent signals agree.

            And when there is nothing to find, we say nothing.

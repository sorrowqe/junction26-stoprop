Check a source

Enter a link or a domain and get plain-English “receipts”: how old the domain is, when it was first archived, its first TLS certificate, whether it looks like a clone of a known outlet, and related fact-checks.

Coming in milestone M3. Receipts that were not checked will say “not checked” instead of being hidden.

Stoprop labels techniques and behaviours, never people, outlets or viewpoints.

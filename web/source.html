Check a source

Enter a link or a domain and get plain-English “receipts”: how old the domain is, when the Internet Archive first saved it, its first TLS certificate, whether it looks like a clone of a known news outlet, and related fact-checks. No AI is used here, and checks that were not run say “not checked”.

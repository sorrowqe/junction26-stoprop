Swarm mode

Spot coordinated amplification and copy-paste campaigns. Paste a social thread or channel link to check its posts and comments — or paste any article link, claim, or viral post text to trace where the same wording spread across the web and public platforms.

We trace where this text spread. We don't research who is behind any account. A group is called “likely coordinated” only when copied text and at least one independent signal (timing burst, account pattern, or shared link push) agree.

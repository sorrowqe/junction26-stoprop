Swarm mode

Upload a batch of posts (author, time, text) and Stoprop looks for coordinated behaviour: text reuse, timing synchrony, account similarity and amplification. It never judges a single post or person. A cluster is called “likely coordinated” only when two independent signals agree.

Coming in milestone M5. A sample dataset with real-looking campaigns and look-alike innocent activity will be included.

Stoprop labels techniques and behaviours, never people, outlets or viewpoints.

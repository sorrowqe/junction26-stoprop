Swarm mode

Paste a link to a public post and Stoprop reads the replies, then looks for coordinated behaviour: copied text, timing bursts, look-alike accounts and pushed links. It never judges a single post or person. A group is called “likely coordinated” only when copied text and at least one independent signal agree.

Posts to check

Stoprop collects the post and its replies (up to 2000 posts, about 25 seconds).

Each link counts as one post (replies are not collected). Use this to check whether posts you found in different places come from a coordinated group.

Format

Columns id, author, timestamp, text (timestamp in ISO 8601 or unix time), optional url, author_created_at, platform. JSON: a list of objects with the same fields. Up to 2000 posts or 2 MB. Rows with problems are skipped and listed.

Options

Which links work?
  • Bluesky: any public post (bsky.app/profile/…/post/…), with all replies and quote posts.
  • Mastodon: a post on any server (…/@user/123…). Many servers share at most 60 replies with visitors who are not logged in; Stoprop says so when that happens.
  • YouTube: a video link (watch, youtu.be, shorts), with its comments.
  • Not possible: X (Twitter), Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok and Reddit do not let outside tools read posts without login or paid or approved API access. Export the posts yourself and use “Upload a file”.

Collected posts are processed in memory and kept for 10 minutes so a repeated check is instant; nothing is written to disk.

Result

        Verdicts describe behaviour in this batch, never people. “Likely coordinated” needs copied text plus timing, account or amplification evidence. Innocent explanations (shared headlines, generic phrases, quoted statements, event hashtags) lower the verdict and are listed with each group. Groups of the same accounts posting several different messages are shown as one campaign.

        Stoprop labels techniques and behaviours, never people, outlets or viewpoints.

Swarm mode

Upload a batch of posts (author, time, text) and Stoprop looks for coordinated behaviour: text reuse, timing synchrony, account similarity and amplification. It never judges a single post or person. A group is called “likely coordinated” only when copied text and at least one independent signal agree.

Your posts

Format

Columns id, author, timestamp, text (timestamp in ISO 8601 or unix time), optional url, author_created_at, platform. JSON: a list of objects with the same fields. Up to 2000 posts or 2 MB. Rows with problems are skipped and listed.

Options

Processed in memory only; nothing you upload is stored.

Result

      Verdicts describe behaviour in this batch, never people. “Likely coordinated” needs copied text plus timing, account or amplification evidence. Innocent explanations (shared headlines, generic phrases, quoted statements, event hashtags) lower the verdict and are listed with each group.

      Stoprop labels techniques and behaviours, never people, outlets or viewpoints.

Trace a post

Start from one post. Stoprop picks its most distinctive sentences, searches for exact copies on the open web and on Bluesky, and then checks the copies for coordinated behaviour.

We trace where this text spread. We don't research who is behind any account. Public search results only; no profile lookups, no linking of accounts across platforms, nothing stored.

Post to trace

Searched: the open web (search engine results) and Bluesky. Not searched: Mastodon (its search needs a login), and X, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit (closed to outside tools). Each trace uses one small AI call to choose sentences and up to 6 searches; 5 traces per hour.
